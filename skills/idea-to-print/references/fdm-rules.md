# FDM rules

Printer default: Anycubic Kobra 3 Max. Nozzle 0.4 mm. Layer 0.2 mm.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Min wall | 1.2 mm (3× nozzle) |
| Press fit | +0.15 mm per side (risk of chip) |
| Slide fit (magnets) | +0.30 mm per side |
| Loose / bearing drop-in | +0.45 mm per side |
| 608 seat | Ø22.2 mm, depth ≥7 mm |
| First print | Fit coupon, not the rotor |

Material for rotors/hubs: PETG or ABS, 5–6 walls, ~40% infill.

Do not generate organic AI meshes (Meshy, Tripo, Rodin). They are non-parametric and fail the no-hand-edit rule.
